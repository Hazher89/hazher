Privacy Policy

ZepRise

Last updated: November 7, 2026
Publisher: Hazher / Hama Hazher (org.nr. 837 568 382)
Contact: support@hazher.no

ZepRise (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a free balloon ascent game for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what limited data may be processed when you play, why we process it, and the choices you have. You can play without creating an email or password account with us.

1. Privacy principles

  • Play first — no mandatory login to us.
  • Collect only what keeps the game free, fair, and working.
  • Never sell your personal information.
  • Prefer on-device storage for progress; use platform services you already control (such as Apple Game Center) when you choose to compete.

2. Information on your device

ZepRise stores gameplay state locally on your iPhone, for example:

  • High meters, run history, pilot XP / level, coin bank, owned skins & trails
  • Boost inventory, settings (sound, haptics, Duo stick side), onboarding & legal acceptance flags

This data stays on your device unless you optionally use Game Center, or until you delete the app (which removes local progress).

3. Anonymous operations metrics

To understand whether the game is healthy — how many devices have opened it, how often people fly, and rough height distribution — we may send anonymous technical signals to our servers (e.g. Supabase):

  • A random install identifier generated on your device (not your name, email, or Apple ID)
  • App version, platform (iOS/Android), coarse timestamps
  • Session open and run-end events (for example meters reached)

These signals are not used to build a marketing profile of you, are not sold, and are not required for you to play.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

ZepRise is free and supported by ads (banner on the home screen, and optional rewarded videos you choose for revive or bonus coins). Google AdMob and its partners may process:

  • Advertising identifiers (subject to your iOS Tracking permission)
  • Device & app info, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data

Apple may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt before personalized ads. You can decline; non-personalized ads may still appear. We do not sell personal information.

5. Optional Apple Game Center

If you use leaderboards or achievements, Apple processes your Game Center nickname and scores under Apple’s privacy terms. Participation is optional and managed in iOS Settings → Game Center.

6. Diagnostics

Apple or your device OS may collect crash and performance diagnostics as part of normal system operation. We do not sell that data.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require an email/password account with ZepRise to play
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing
  • We do not sell personal data

8. Children’s privacy

ZepRise is a casual arcade game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region) for commercial purposes. Because the game shows third-party ads, it is not submitted as a “Made for Kids” experience. Parents may contact support@hazher.no.

9. Your choices

  • Disable sound and haptics in Settings
  • Reset local progress by deleting the app
  • Manage Game Center in iOS Settings
  • Manage tracking / Apple Advertising under Privacy & Security

10. International processing & retention

Anonymous ops metrics and ad partners may process data on servers outside your country. We retain anonymous aggregates only as long as useful for operating and improving ZepRise.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy, except where local law requires a fresh consent.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: support@hazher.no
