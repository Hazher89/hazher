Ali Barber — support

Ali Barber Woburn Sands is the booking app for the salon in Woburn Sands, UK. For questions about the app, your account, or technical issues, email kontakt@hazher.no. For a specific appointment or treatment, contact the salon directly.

Contact

App & technical support: kontakt@hazher.no

Salon / bookings: Ali Barber Woburn Sands — visit the salon in person or call the number shown in the app.

Sign-in and account

Sign in with Apple or Google. Your profile stores your name and email from your sign-in provider. You can delete your account under Profile → Delete Account. If you change phones, use the same Apple or Google account.

Booking appointments

Choose a service, date, and time. Pick a specific stylist or select "Any Barber" for automatic assignment. Times outside opening hours or without available capacity are not shown as bookable.

Cancellations

Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment where the app allows it. Late cancellations may incur a fee in line with the salon's policies (see Terms in the app).

Push notifications

Enable notifications when the app asks — then in your phone's settings for Ali Barber. You will receive confirmations, reminders, and updates about schedule changes.

Admin (salon staff)

Command Center provides access to the schedule, opening hours, stylists, smart scheduling, and users. Contact a super admin if you need a new role or access level.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot find any available times

Check that the salon is open on that day (opening hours are shown in the app). All stylists may be fully booked — try "Any Barber" or another date.

Push notifications are not working

Go to Profile and enable notifications, then iOS Settings → Ali Barber → Notifications. Make sure you are signed in and connected to the internet.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account in the app, or use our web form at hazher.no/ALIBARBER/Delete/ to request deletion by email.

Where can I find privacy and terms?

In the app at sign-in and under Profile, and on hazher.no/ALIBARBER/Privacy/.
