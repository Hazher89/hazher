Account Data deletion

Delete your Ali Barber account

You can request that your Ali Barber Woburn Sands account and associated personal data are deleted. Use the in-app option when possible, or email us using the link below.

Account deletion URLhttps://hazher.no/ALIBARBER/Delete/
Request deletion by email Privacy Policy

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Ali Barber app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, and booking data linked to the app, subject to legal retention where required.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email kontakt@hazher.no with:

  • The subject line: Delete account – Ali Barber
  • Your full name
  • The email address used to sign in (Apple or Google)
  • Whether you used Apple or Google Sign-In

Open email request

What we delete

  • Account profile (name, email linked to the app)
  • Bookings and related in-app history
  • Push notification device tokens
  • Other personal data stored for the app service

We may keep limited records where required by law (for example accounting or dispute resolution). Apple or Google account data held by those providers is managed under their own policies.

Processing time

In-app deletion is usually immediate. Email requests are typically completed within 30 days of verifying your identity. You will receive a confirmation when done.
